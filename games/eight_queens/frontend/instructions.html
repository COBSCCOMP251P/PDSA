How to Play

Learn the Eight Queens puzzle in 2 minutes

The Challenge

Place 8 queens on a standard chessboard so that no queen can attack another. In chess, queens can move horizontally, vertically, and diagonally — so your placement must avoid all these lines of attack.

92 unique solutions exist

The Rules

  • Place exactly 8 queens on the 8×8 board
  • No two queens can share the same row
  • No two queens can share the same column
  • No two queens can share the same diagonal

How to Play

  • Click any empty square to place a queen
  • Click a queen to remove it
  • Red highlighting indicates conflicts
  • Use hints if you get stuck
  • Submit when all 8 queens are safely placed

Scoring

  • Base points depend on difficulty (50/100/200)
  • Time bonus: +1 point for each second under the limit
  • Hint penalty: −10 points per hint used
  • Discovery bonus: +50 for finding a new unique solution
