Traveling Salesman

Plan the Best Route

Build your route, compare it against algorithms, and see how close you get to the optimal Traveling Salesman path.

Ready to Play?

The home city and distance matrix are random each round. You pick the cities to visit, plan your order, and then see how your path stacks up against brute force, nearest neighbor, and dynamic programming solvers.

Quick Tutorial

  1. Generate the matrix to reveal the random home city (cannot be selected).
  2. Select the other cities you want to visit.
  3. Choose the visit order (home is fixed at start/end).
  4. Run the game to compare your route vs. Brute Force, NN, and DP.
  5. Results auto-save; play the next round or start a new game.
